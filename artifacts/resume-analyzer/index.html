Frequently Asked Questions — CV Audit Tool

What is a CV Audit?
A CV audit is a systematic review of your resume against a specific job description, evaluating keyword relevance, ATS compatibility, formatting, and content quality. CV Audit Tool automates this process using AI — delivering a complete resume audit report in seconds.
What is an ATS Score?
An ATS score is a percentage that shows how closely your resume matches a job description as scored by an Applicant Tracking System. Most companies set a minimum ATS threshold (typically 70–80%). Our AI resume analyzer calculates your ATS score and shows you exactly how to improve it.
